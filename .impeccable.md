# Craft 12-17 — Impeccable Design Context

This file persists design context for all future sessions on this project. It is derived from `design.md` (the locked April 2026 design brief) and the codebase as it stands.

## Design Context

### Users

**Primary audience:** Lacey Lee's existing salon clientele — women who already know and trust Moda Organic Salon & Spa next door. They are the warmest possible audience for Craft 12-17 (a new neighborhood cafe at 5325 NE 4th St, Renton, WA 98059). Secondary: anyone who walks/drives past or finds the page via local search.

**Context of use:** A one-shot visit. Probably arriving from a salon flyer, an Instagram link, or a Google search for the address. They are not "shopping" — they are checking whether a thing they already heard about is real. The page exists to capture an email so Lacey can write them when the doors open.

**Job to be done:** "Tell me this is real, tell me when it opens, and don't make me work for it." The visitor leaves their email and never thinks about the page again until the launch announcement.

**Emotional goal:** *Warmth and quiet anticipation.* Like walking past a cafe that hasn't opened yet, peering in the window, and seeing the chairs already arranged. Not hype. Not a countdown. The page should feel like a personal note, not a marketing landing page.

### Brand Personality

Three words: **soft, considered, feminine** (in the editorial-magazine sense — not the wedding-invitation sense).

Voice: lowercase, unhurried, declarative. "opening soon" not "OPENING SOON!" or "Coming Soon — Get Notified Today!". The copy whispers; the typography carries the volume.

Brand origin: the color palette is pulled from a bouquet of roses. The page should feel like a bouquet itself — soft petals of color against cream, charcoal as the stems. Restraint is the entire point.

The brand has a sister relationship with Moda Organic Salon & Spa (literally — the cafe is next door, same owner). The "a sister to moda" tag in the top-left corner acknowledges this without being subordinate to it.

### Aesthetic Direction

**Direction A — "editorial whisper"** was selected over directions B (modern atelier) and C (soft handcrafted). What that means in practice:

- Cream-led page, not a pink page. Pink is jewelry, not paint. Proportion rule: ~70% cream `#F5F2E8`, ~20% charcoal `#373739`, ~10% pinks.
- Three pinks, each with one job: rose `#EF5D7A` carries the brand (wordmark date, CTA), blush `#E59C99` supports (petal fills, hover state), coral `#FF7C8B` celebrates (success state only). Never let coral appear before submission.
- Display type is Fraunces italic (high-contrast, fashion-magazine masthead energy). Body type is Inter (it disappears so the serif and the cream can breathe).
- Composition is centered and *near*-symmetric. The page leans on content asymmetry (corner labels, the floral mark, the directional arrow on the CTA) to keep it modern instead of bridal.

**Anti-references — what this is NOT:**
- Not a typical coming-soon page with a countdown clock.
- Not a hero-image build (no photography yet — committing to stock would dilute the brand).
- Not a "fully launched" preview. No nav, no footer links, no social grid, no menu tease.
- Not a literal pink page. Not bridal. Not bakery-cute.
- Not a brand reveal disconnected from Moda — the sister tag is intentional warmest-leads strategy.

**Theme:** Light only. Cream `#F5F2E8` is the page; charcoal `#373739` is the type. No dark-mode variant is planned for the coming-soon phase. (The admin area uses a separate dark theme via the existing `ff.*` Tailwind tokens; do not confuse the two palettes.)

### Design Principles

1. **Restraint is the brand.** Every addition should be challenged. If a flourish doesn't earn its place, it dilutes the rest. The page does *one* job.
2. **Pink discipline.** Three pinks, three jobs (rose=brand, blush=support, coral=celebration). Never let them blur into each other.
3. **Cream carries the weight.** ~70% cream, ~20% charcoal, ~10% pink. Pink is jewelry. If a new element makes the page feel pinker overall, push pink back out.
4. **Content asymmetry, not visual asymmetry.** The composition is centered. Modernity comes from corner labels, the floral mark above the wordmark, the arrow on the CTA — not from breaking the center axis.
5. **Type carries the volume; copy whispers.** Lowercase, unhurried, declarative. The Fraunces italic is the loudest thing on the page. Body copy never asks for attention.
6. **One color animation.** The input-underline shift from charcoal to rose on focus. This is the only animated color change the page earns. Do not add more.
7. **Don't fake content.** No countdown, no fake testimonials, no stock cafe photos, no fabricated social proof. The page is honest about its holding-pattern role.
